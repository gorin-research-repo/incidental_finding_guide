    Clinical use note

    This tool supports—not replaces—clinical judgment. Comorbidities, prior cancer, immunosuppression, symptoms, interval change, and patient preferences may alter management. Seek radiology or subspecialty input for complex scenarios.

    About this guide and methodology

    This quick-reference guide synthesizes organ-specific incidental-finding guidelines from multiple professional societies. Where recommendations differed slightly, the most widely adopted thresholds were used. It covers common findings but is not exhaustive.

    Contrast guidance reflects the imaging protocols in those guidelines, supplemented by ACR Appropriateness Criteria and current NCCN recommendations. Individual radiology consultation may be appropriate.
